Friday text alerts: in-app opt-in

Friday is a business assistant software application operated by Jerad Watson, sole proprietor. It sends automated account alerts by text to account holders who turn texts on inside the application.

Opt-in method: in-app, behind a login. Consent is collected only on the Settings, Texts screen of the Friday application, which an account holder reaches after signing in with a passkey. It is not a website opt-in: there is no public page or form to opt in from and no keyword opt-in. Because the screen requires a login, this page hosts screenshots and a screen recording of the complete consent flow for review.

I agree to receive automated account alerts from Friday by text at this number: one-time verification codes, deadline reminders and alerts about urgent business messages. Message frequency varies. The confirmation text

Friday: your verification code is [6-digit code]. Enter it on the Texting screen in the Friday app to confirm this number. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

Messages, frequency, opt-out

Automated account alerts generated by the application: one-time verification codes, reminders when a tracked business deadline is near, and alerts when urgent business email or account activity needs attention. Message frequency varies, typically one to a few per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to cancel, HELP for help. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No marketing.
