# Friday Texting Service: Terms and Conditions

Effective October 22, 2026.

**Program description.** Friday, a business assistant application operated by Jerad Watson, sole proprietor, sends automated account alerts by text: one-time verification codes, reminders when a tracked business deadline is near, and alerts when urgent business email or account activity needs attention. Messages are generated by the application; no marketing is sent.

**Opt-in.** By entering a mobile number on the Texting screen in the Friday application, ticking the optional consent box and confirming the one-time code, the account holder agrees to receive these automated text messages from Friday at that number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

**Message frequency.** Message frequency varies. Typically one to a few messages per day.

**Message and data rates may apply.** Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

**Opt-out.** Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one confirmation text and no further messages unless you opt in again.

**Help.** Reply HELP to any message for help, or use the contact method below.

**Carriers.** Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

**Changes.** These terms may be updated. The current version is always available at this page, and the effective date above will change.

**Contact.** Reply HELP to any message you have received from this service, or contact the operator through the Friday application. Operator: Jerad Watson, sole proprietor, Texas, United States.
