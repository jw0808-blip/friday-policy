Opt-in flow for text messages from Friday, operated by Jerad Watson, sole proprietor. This is the sign-up step exactly as it appears on the Texting section of the Friday dashboard Settings page. The dashboard is a private, single-user application behind a login, so the step is published here for review. Friday texts one person only: the account owner, who signs up himself with this form.

## Step 1. Enter your mobile number

Mobile number: `+1 555 555 5555`

Only the account owner's own number can be entered. No one else can be added, and nothing is sent until step 3 is finished.

## Step 2. Agree to receive texts (optional, unchecked by default)

☐ I agree to receive automated text messages from Friday (Jerad Watson, sole proprietor) at the mobile number above. Messages include a short morning summary, reminders I have set, and one-time confirmation codes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) and [Terms and Conditions](terms.md).

[ Enable texts ] (stays off until the box is ticked; the dashboard works without texts)

## Step 3. Confirm your number

After tapping Enable texts, Friday sends one text to the number entered:

> Friday: your code to enable texts is 482913. Enter it on the Settings page to finish. Reply STOP to end texts, HELP for help.

Code from the text: `6-digit code`  [ Confirm ]

Texting turns on only after the correct code is entered. The Settings page then shows "Texts on" with a Turn off button, and the privacy policy and terms stay linked there.

## What you will get

A short morning summary around 7:00 AM, reminders you have set, alerts you asked for, and one-time confirmation codes. Typically one to a few texts per day. No marketing and no messages to anyone but you.

## How to stop or get help

Reply STOP to any message to cancel; you get one confirmation and nothing more. Reply HELP for help. You can also tap Turn off on the Settings page.
