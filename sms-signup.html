Friday texting opt-in

Friday is a business assistant software application operated by Jerad Watson, sole proprietor. It sends automated account alerts by text. Account holders opt in on the Texting screen inside the Friday application, which requires a login, so the screen is reproduced below with every disclosure for carrier review. Opt-in method: authenticated web form. There is no public sign-up form and no keyword opt-in.

Step 1

Sign in, open Settings, Texting, and enter a mobile number

Nothing is sent until step 3 is finished.

Step 2

Agree to receive texts (optional, unchecked by default)

The Enable texts button stays off until the box is ticked. You can leave the box unticked and keep using the application without texts.

Step 3

Confirm your number

After you tap Enable texts, Friday sends one text to the number you entered:

Friday: your verification code is 482913. Enter it on the Texting screen in the Friday app to confirm this number. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

Texting turns on only after the correct code is entered. The Texting screen then shows Texts on with a Turn texts off button, and the privacy policy and terms stay linked there.

What you will get

Automated account alerts generated by the Friday application: one-time verification codes, reminders when a tracked business deadline is near, and alerts when urgent business email or account activity needs attention. Message frequency varies, typically one to a few per day. No marketing.

Examples:
Friday deadline alert: [deadline name] for [business name] is due in [hours] hours. Open the Friday app to review. Reply STOP to cancel.
Friday account alert: [count] new emails marked urgent in your [business name] inbox, from [sender]. Details in the Friday app. Reply STOP to cancel.

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP to any message to cancel; you get one confirmation and nothing more. Reply HELP for help. You can also tap Turn texts off on the Texting screen.
